Aspect Ratio Bundles

Smart Crop + Edge Fill

Smart Crop now crops evenly when only a small crop is needed. If too much would be cropped, it preserves the full artwork and fills the edges with a softened extension of the image instead of white borders. Use Standard Crop for full-bleed files, or Preserve entire image when you do not want any cropping at all.

Expanded Etsy Size Strategy

This app includes expanded Etsy-style print packages: 2:3 up to 24x36; 3:4 up to 24x32; 4:5 up to 24x30; 11x14 up to 22x28; and ISO International A5 through A1, plus 5x7. The Etsy ZIP places files into matching ratio folders.

Smart Fit Orientation Note

Landscape mode now swaps the actual export dimensions. Example: 24x36 exports as 10800 × 7200 pixels for landscape artwork. Use Smart fit recommended to prevent mismatched sizes from being badly cropped. The app will fill matching orientations and add margins when the orientation does not match. Bundle buttons now add to your current selection instead of clearing previous selections.

Recommended Etsy Size Strategy

This app now uses the most common printable wall art ratio packages: 2:3 for 4x6, 8x12, 12x18, 16x24, 24x36; 3:4 for 6x8, 9x12, 12x16, 18x24; 4:5 for 8x10, 11x14, 16x20; and ISO for A1, A2, A3, A4. The Etsy ZIP will place files into matching ratio folders.

Recommended Etsy Size Strategy

For printable wall art, your current size set is strong. The most useful bundles are 2:3, 3:4, 4:5, and common portrait sizes like 5x7, 8x10, 11x14, 16x20, 18x24, and 24x36. A-series sizes are helpful for international buyers, but they are optional. If you want to keep your shop simple, you can skip A sizes at first and add them later if customers ask.
